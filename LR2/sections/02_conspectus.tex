\subsection{What are the objectives of the coursework?}

\begin{enumerate}
\item To write symbolic math expressions for continuous-time signals and specific filter impulse responses;
\item To successfully sketch multiple sequences demonstrating time-domain operations like shifting, flipping, and compression;
\end{enumerate}	
	
\subsection{How does the coursework fit the course and previously done coursework?}
By:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Applying practical MATLAB programming to execute theoretical signal operations covered in the Signals and Systems course.
\item Translating symbolic math expressions into visual time-domain representations for deeper behavioral analysis.
\end{enumerate}	

\subsection{How were the objectives achieved?}
By:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Utilizing MATLAB functions such as heaviside, dirac, and exp to symbolically define the input signals and filter responses.
\item Using plotting commands like subplot to arrange multiple graphs into a single figure and xcorr to determine the auto-correlation.
\end{enumerate}
	
\subsection{What are the key results and generalizations?}
The key results are:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Time-domain operations were visually verified, showing accurate signal shifts, reversals, and scaling based on their mathematical adjustments.
\item The impulse responses for various filters were successfully plotted, and the auto-correlation effectively displayed the relationship between the signal's amplitude and lag $\tau$.
\end{enumerate}
