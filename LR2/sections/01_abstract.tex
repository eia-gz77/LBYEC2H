This report summarizes Laboratory Exercise 2, which focuses on the time-domain representation of signals and their corresponding operations. The primary objectives include determining symbolic mathematical expressions for continuous-time signals and utilizing MATLAB to sketch these sequences accurately. Essential signal operations such as time shifting, time reversal, and time compression are applied to a base signal to visually demonstrate their theoretical effects in the time domain. Furthermore, the coursework explores the mathematical formulation and plotting of impulse responses for standard filters, specifically low-pass, high-pass, bandpass, and bandstop filters. Finally, the auto-correlation of a specified signal is computed and graphed against its lag time to analyze its self-similarity.
